## What it is

Today a koinon's muster fights at its launch instant, and only the way home takes time. From now on the army travels, as a party has since raids prompt 4.

1. The launch. At the launch instant everything is decided as today: the members who still count (P10), each owner's settle, the pledged men and hulls, the stand-downs, the hulls that sail and the seats they fill. An army that marches sets out: every pledged row is on the road to the target on the march clock (30 minutes a step), and the hulls that sail leave port for the round trip (twice the road). Nothing is fought yet.
2. The arrival. The battle is fought at the arrival instant against the place as it stands then, resolved by the first request after that instant, in arrival order with the parties' marches. Everything is dated the arrival: the pool and its regrowth, the town's fleet, the altar, the plunder and its split, the grudge, the intel, the Chronicle lines and the report. The survivors take the same road home.
3. Turned back, or lost on the road. An army that arrives at a place one of its own houses now holds turns back without a fight. An army whose men all left the roster on the road (unpaid upkeep, a band's contract ending) breaks up: no fight. Men who left on the road do not fight and earn no share; hulls that sailed keep their seats' shares. A member who leaves the koinon while the army marches still fights and takes his share. Winter stops launches only: an army already on the road arrives and fights.
4. One muster at a time. While its army marches, a koinon cannot call another muster. The Muster card shows the army on the march: where it is bound, when it arrives, and who sent what.
5. Naval supplies. A muster's hulls take naval supplies as every other voyage does (barracks prompt 4): each hull that sails takes its `suppliesPerTrip` for the voyage there and back. A member who pledges hulls to a muster that goes by sea must hold the supplies for his pledged hulls that can make the crossing, or the pledge is refused with the usual sentence ("Not enough naval supplies: 2 needed, 1 in store."). At the launch each owner's sailing hulls take their supplies from his stock. A member who has spent his since he pledged still sails, and pays what he has left. By land no hull sails and nothing is taken.
6. The muster's report goes to the Barracks. Every member who took part finds it in his Barracks Reports, with his own parties' reports and the same highlight until he first opens it. It also stays on the koinon page as the last muster, with one Chronicle line for each member who took part. A muster that stood down at its launch sent no one and writes no Barracks report.
7. The Reports list is simpler. Each report is one short title, such as "Raided Edetani" or "Scouted Salyes", with its NEW highlight and its kind's tag. Everything else (the full line, the numbers, the date) is in the card it opens. The list is a box like Away · Returning and In training, under them in the right-hand column, and it says so when it is empty.

Rulings already made (9 Oct 2026): musters march and fight when they arrive; their hulls take naval supplies, one per hull per voyage; a muster's report reaches every member who took part in his Barracks; and the Reports list shows only a title per report, in a box like Away · Returning and In training, with the rest in the card.
